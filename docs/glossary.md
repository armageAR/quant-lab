# Glossary

- **MarketDataProvider**: abstraction that supplies quotes, candles, trades, and related market data.
- **Strategy**: a unit of trading logic that consumes data and produces signals or orders.
- **Signal**: a strategy output indicating intent, direction, or confidence.
- **Paper trading**: simulated execution using live or historical data without sending real orders.
- **Live execution**: order routing to a real venue or broker.
- **CCXT**: connector library used for exchange integration.
- **Venue**: exchange or broker where orders are sent.
- **IBKR**: Interactive Brokers, a future broker integration target.
